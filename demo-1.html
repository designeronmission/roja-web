What Is Roja

The Technology Layer Powering Earned Wage Access

Roja is not a consumer-facing app and not a financial product. Roja is the technology layer that powers Earned Wage Access programs for organizations that want to offer earned-access capabilities while retaining full brand ownership, operational control, and alignment with internal compliance frameworks.

The platform is designed to support earned-access use cases across multiple operating models, jurisdictions, and partner structures—without dictating how programs must be branded or delivered.

Retain full brand ownership and control

Align with internal compliance frameworks

Support multiple operating models and jurisdictions

Our Technology

Built for Earned-Access Models & Enterprise-Grade Infrastructure

Roja is designed to support earned-access programs across a variety of structures and deployment approaches with enterprise-grade infrastructure.

Flexible Earned-Access Models

Roja is purpose-built to support a wide range of earned-access structures, including Earned Wage Access (EWA), no-cost access models, cash-flow-based access programs, and employer-sponsored or consumer-initiated earned access.

API-First Architecture

Modular, API-driven components designed to integrate seamlessly with existing systems and operational processes. All components are configurable and built for flexibility.

Secure Data Handling & Compliance

Configurable compliance and disclosure controls with secure data handling and audit readiness built into the platform architecture.

Enterprise-Grade Reliability

Built to support enterprise operating environments where scalability, security, and reliability are essential. Designed to integrate into existing technology stacks without requiring system replacements.
