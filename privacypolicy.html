1. Scope of This Privacy Policy

ROJA (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is a technology platform provider. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you interact with our website, applications, and platform.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly, information collected automatically through your use of the platform, and information processed on behalf of business partners under contractual obligations.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used to operate and improve the platform, provide support, ensure security, and comply with legal obligations. ROJA does not sell personal information.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We may share information with service providers, partners, or authorities as required by law. All sharing is limited to operational necessity.

5. Third-Party Services

ROJA integrates with third-party service providers. These providers operate under their own privacy policies.

6. Data Security

ROJA applies industry-standard security practices including encryption, access controls, and system monitoring. Absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

7. Data Retention

Personal information is retained only as long as necessary for business or legal purposes.

8. Your Rights

Depending on jurisdiction, individuals may have rights to access, correct, or delete their personal information.

9. Children's Privacy

ROJA does not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 18.

10. International Data Transfers

Information may be processed in jurisdictions where ROJA or its service providers operate.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically with changes posted on this page.

12. Contact Us

13. California Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

This section applies to California residents and supplements the information above.

13.a. Categories of Personal Information Collected

Identifiers, internet activity information, and professional or business-related information may be collected. ROJA does not knowingly collect sensitive personal information beyond what is necessary.

13.b. Purposes for Collection

Personal information is collected for platform operation, support, security, and compliance purposes.

13.c. Sale or Sharing of Personal Information

ROJA does not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

13.d. Retention

Personal information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary.

13.e. California Consumer Rights

California residents have rights to know, delete, correct, limit, and not be discriminated against.

13.f. Exercising Your Rights

Requests may be submitted to support@roja.one and will be verified.

13.g. Service Provider Disclosure

ROJA acts as a service provider when processing data on behalf of partners.

13.h. Updates

This California notice may be updated periodically.
