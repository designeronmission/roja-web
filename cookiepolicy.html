1. What Are Cookies and Similar Technologies?

This Cookie Policy explains how Roja ("Company," "we," "our," or "us") uses cookies and similar technologies in the Roja mobile application (the "App"), website at rojaone.com (the "Site"), and related services (together, the "Services").

1.1. Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website or app to store preferences, enable functionality, or track usage.

1.2. SDKs and mobile identifiers

In mobile apps, software development kits ("SDKs") and device identifiers (such as Apple's IDFA or Google's GAID) function similarly to cookies, enabling analytics and advertising.

1.3. Other technologies

We may also use:

  • Pixels or web beacons: tiny images embedded in emails or webpages to measure engagement.
  • Local storage / session storage: browser or app-based storage of small data snippets.
  • Fingerprinting technologies: methods of identifying a device/browser using technical characteristics (used only in compliance with law).

2. Types of Cookies We Use

We categorize cookies and similar technologies as follows:

2.1. Strictly Necessary Cookies

Essential for the operation of the Services.
Examples: authentication tokens, load balancing, fraud prevention.
Cannot be disabled without impairing functionality.

2.2. Functional / Preference Cookies

Remember your choices (language, region, login state).
Provide a more personalized user experience.

2.3. Performance / Analytics Cookies

Collect anonymous or pseudonymous usage data.
Help us understand how users interact with the App and Site.
Examples: Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Mixpanel.

2.4. Advertising / Targeting Cookies

Track user interactions across the App/Site and third-party platforms.
Enable delivery of personalized ads and measurement of ad performance.
Examples: Google Ads, Meta (Facebook/Instagram), affiliate tracking cookies.

2.5. Social Media Cookies

Allow content sharing via social media platforms.
May track your activity across multiple sites.

3. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Authenticate users and secure logins;
  • Store session information and prevent fraud;
  • Measure site and app performance;
  • Analyze usage to improve features and services;
  • Deliver advertising, including personalized ads;
  • Track engagement with sponsored offers;
  • Provide social media integration.

4. Legal Basis for Use of Cookies

4.1. U.S. users (CCPA/CPRA)

Cookies may be considered "personal information."
Sharing cookie data with ad networks may constitute "sale" or "sharing" under CPRA.
You have the right to opt-out (see Section 7).

4.2. Other state laws

Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, and Utah provide similar rights to opt out of targeted advertising or profiling. Roja honors these opt-out rights through the mechanisms described in Section 7.

5. Third-Party Cookies

5.1. Analytics providers

Google Analytics, Firebase, and similar providers may set cookies or SDK identifiers to analyze user behavior.

5.2. Advertising partners

Ad networks (e.g., Google Ads, Meta Ads) may set cookies to deliver targeted ads and measure engagement.

5.3. Social media platforms

If you use share buttons or social logins, social media companies may set cookies to track interactions.

5.4. Connector providers

Financial account connectors may use cookies or tokens for secure authentication and session management.

6. Cookie Duration

6.1. Session cookies

Expire when you close your browser or log out of the App.

6.2. Persistent cookies

Remain on your device until they expire or are deleted manually. Durations range from a few days to two years, depending on purpose and provider.

7. Your Choices and Controls

7.1. Opt-out (U.S. users)

California residents can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information by clicking the "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link available in the Site footer and App settings.
Residents of other states with similar rights (Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah) can exercise equivalent opt-out choices via the same link.

7.2. Browser/device controls

Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies.
Mobile devices (iOS/Android) allow you to limit ad tracking.

7.3. Impact of disabling cookies

Disabling strictly necessary cookies may prevent use of some features.
Disabling analytics or advertising cookies will not affect core functionality but may reduce personalization.

8. Managing SDKs in Mobile Apps

8.1. In-app settings

Roja provides controls to disable analytics or ad personalization in the App's privacy settings (where supported).

8.2. Platform settings

On iOS, you may use "App Tracking Transparency" (ATT) to deny tracking.
On Android, you may reset your GAID (Google Advertising ID) or limit ad personalization.

9. Do Not Track (DNT) Signals

Our Site does not currently respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals, as no consistent industry standard has been adopted. We honor opt-out rights provided by California and other U.S. state laws through the mechanisms described in Section 7.

10. Updates to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, regulation, or our practices. Material changes will be communicated via our Site, App, or email (if required).
