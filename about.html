White-Labeled by Design

Your Brand Comes First

Roja is designed as a white-labeled technology platform that enables organizations to offer EWA capabilities under their own brand. The platform supports configurable user experiences and operational workflows while allowing partners to define how EWA programs are presented, administered, and governed within their own frameworks. Roja does not present itself to end users and does not control branding, customer relationships, or program positioning. These elements remain with the deploying organization. Roja operates in the background as a technology provider, supporting program delivery without assuming ownership of the product or the end-user relationship. Each Roja deployment supports the ability to:

Present the experience entirely under the partner’s brand
 

Configure program settings, disclosures, and user interaction flows

Maintain direct relationships with end users
 

Align program operation with internal policies, risk frameworks, and governance standards

Who Roja Is Built For

Roja is built for organizations that want to offer EWA capabilities without developing and maintaining the underlying technology themselves. The platform is intended for teams that value brand ownership, operational control, and flexibility, and that require a solution capable of supporting EWA programs across different operating models and growth stages. Roja is designed for organizations that want to:

Launch EWA programs under their own brand
 

Reduce internal development time and complexity
 

Maintain control over program design and operations
 

Offer modern EWA experiences responsibly and at scale
 

How the Platform Works

Roja provides a configurable technology framework that supports the end-to-end operation of EWA programs. The platform is designed to handle the core functional components required to deliver EWA experiences while allowing organizations to integrate these capabilities into their existing systems, workflows, and operational environments. Roja does not prescribe business models or user interactions; instead, it enables partners to define how programs are structured, delivered, and managed. All components are modular, API-driven, and designed to integrate seamlessly with existing systems and operational processes. Roja delivers the core components required to support EWA programs, including:

Branded mobile and web experiences
 

Income and bank verification workflows
 

Eligibility and access logic
 

Repayment and reconciliation tools
 

Configurable compliance and disclosure controls
 

Enterprise-Grade Infrastructure

Roja is built to support enterprise operating environments where scalability, security, and reliability are essential. The platform is designed to integrate into existing technology stacks and operational processes while supporting consistent performance and structured oversight as programs grow. Its architecture emphasizes flexibility and control without requiring partners to replace their current systems. This allows partners to deploy EWA programs efficiently while maintaining confidence in performance, controls, and oversight. Roja is engineered for scale, security, and operational reliability:

API-first architecture
