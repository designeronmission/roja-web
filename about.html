How it Works

Empowering the Future of
Earned-Access Technology

At Roja, we're transforming financial access through technology that makes earned-access programs faster, smarter, and more seamless.

What Is Roja

Roja is not a consumer-facing app and not a financial product

Roja is the technology layer that powers Earned Wage Access programs for organizations that want to offer earned-access capabilities while retaining full brand ownership, operational control, and alignment with internal compliance frameworks. The platform is designed to support earned-access use cases across multiple operating models, jurisdictions, and partner structures—without dictating how programs must be branded or delivered.

White-Labeled by Design

Your Brand Comes First

Roja is designed as a white-labeled technology platform that enables organizations to offer earned-access capabilities under their own brand. The platform supports configurable user experiences and operational workflows while allowing partners to define how earned-access programs are presented, administered, and governed within their own frameworks. Roja does not present itself to end users and does not control branding, customer relationships, or program positioning. These elements remain with the deploying organization.

Each Roja deployment supports the ability to:

Present the experience entirely under the partner’s brand

Configure program settings, disclosures, and user interaction flows

Maintain direct relationships with end users
 

Align program operation with internal policies, risk frameworks, and governance standards

  • Roja operates in the background as a technology provider, supporting program delivery without assuming ownership of the product or the end-user relationship.

Who Roja Is Built For

Roja is built for organizations that want to offer earned-access capabilities without developing and maintaining the underlying technology themselves. The platform is intended for teams that value brand ownership, operational control, and flexibility, and that require a solution capable of supporting earned-access programs across different operating models and growth stages.

Roja is designed for organizations that want to:

Launch earned-access programs under their own brand

Reduce internal development time and complexity

Maintain control over program design and operations

Offer modern earned-access experiences responsibly and at scale

How the Platform Works

Roja provides a configurable technology framework that supports the end-to-end operation of Earned Wage Access programs. The platform is designed to handle the core functional components required to deliver earned-access experiences while allowing organizations to integrate these capabilities into their existing systems, workflows, and operational environments. Roja does not prescribe business models or user interactions; instead, it enables partners to define how programs are structured, delivered, and managed.

Roja delivers the core components required to support Earned Wage Access programs, including:

Branded mobile and web experiences
 

Income and bank verification workflows
 

Eligibility and access logic
 

Repayment and reconciliation tools
 

Configurable compliance and disclosure controls

  • All components are modular, API-driven, and designed to integrate seamlessly with existing systems and operational processes.

Built for Earned-Access Models

Roja is designed to support earned-access programs across a variety of structures and deployment approaches. The platform provides configurable technology components that allow organizations to implement earned-access use cases in a manner aligned with their operating models, internal policies, and applicable requirements. Roja does not impose a fixed program structure, allowing partners to define how earned access is offered and managed.

Roja is purpose-built to support a wide range of earned-access structures, including:

Earned Wage Access (EWA)
 

No-cost access models
 

Cash-flow-based access programs
 

Employer-sponsored or consumer-initiated earned access

  • The platform is flexible by design, enabling partners to tailor program rules, eligibility logic, and user experiences to their specific operational and regulatory requirements.

Enterprise-Grade Infrastructure

Roja is built to support enterprise operating environments where scalability, security, and reliability are essential. The platform is designed to integrate into existing technology stacks and operational processes while supporting consistent performance and structured oversight as programs grow. Its architecture emphasizes flexibility and control without requiring partners to replace their current systems. Roja is engineered for scale, security, and operational reliability:

API-first architecture
 

LMS-agnostic integration
 

Configurable program rules and workflows
 

Secure data handling and audit readiness
 

  • This allows partners to deploy earned-access programs efficiently while maintaining confidence in performance, controls, and oversight.
