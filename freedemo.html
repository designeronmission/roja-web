How ROJA Works

App-First. Agentless. Automated.

1. Mobile App Onboarding

Consumers onboard digitally through branded iOS and Android apps using secure open-banking connections—no agents, no paperwork.

2. Subscription Model

Recurring subscriptions replace loan-only monetization, stabilizing revenue and improving customer lifetime value.

3. Automated Advances

Disbursements, repayments, and servicing are automated end-to-end, reducing operational overhead and improving efficiency.

4. Marketplace Revenue

A native marketplace inside the app generates incremental non-loan revenue without additional acquisition spend.
