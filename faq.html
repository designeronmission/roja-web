General Overview

ROJA is a turnkey, app-based B2B technology platform designed for lenders and financial service providers to launch and manage subscription-based, no-cost advance and Earned Wage Access (EWA) products.

ROJA is designed for payday and alternative lenders, tribal and non-bank financial institutions, fintech startups, and existing lenders seeking to modernize away from APR-based products.

No. ROJA is not a lender and does not provide funds directly to consumers. ROJA is a technology platform that enables partners to design and manage their own products.

ROJA helps lenders reduce compliance friction, modernize operations, launch consumer-friendly products faster, and move away from traditional loan-based revenue models.

Product & Technology

ROJA supports no-cost advances, EWA products, subscription-based access programs, and hybrid financial service models.

ROJA is subscription-first, supports non-credit structures, and emphasizes recurring engagement rather than loan servicing and collections.
